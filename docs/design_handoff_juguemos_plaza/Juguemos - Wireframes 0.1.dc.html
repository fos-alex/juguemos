1Juguemos 0.1 · wireframes, Plaza guidelines

Greyscale, 390 px frames. Sign-up is now real — Google or email with an OTP (1a, 1r, 1s) — and Home has a menu opening the side panel (1t). Beyond section 7 of the brief, four more things are pinned here: the mic placeholder and its WhatsApp-style gesture (1b, 1p), an opt-out from the conversational onboarding straight to the form (1b → 1e), "Empezar" with a dismissible timer (1q), and Home showing the kids only — the pet turns up inside activities and stories, not in the header. Structure and copy are real; every tone of grey stands in for a Plaza token (black = jacarandá primary, outline = secondary, flat grey = surface). Two alternatives for Home (1f / 1g) and for Activity (1i / 1j); one each for everything else. No mascot, no sound, no illustration — per the research guardrails.

Ocultar el reloj
Terminamos
The timer exists to get the phone out of the parent's hand. Tapping "Empezar" replaces the instructions with the count and one line that says what to do with the phone: put it down.
  • Big enough to read across a living room, so a glance is enough — no need to pick the phone up.
  • Dismissible without cost. "Ocultar el reloj" returns to the activity with the count still running as a small figure in the header. "Terminamos" ends it, and the app says nothing about how long they actually played.
  • Counts down from the activity's own estimate (15 min). It's a hint, not a target to beat.
  • Silent at zero — no alarm, no chime, no vibration pattern that the toddler will come running to. The screen just stops counting.
  • Back out and the timer survives; it belongs to the activity, not the screen.
Guardrail risk: a timer is the closest this app gets to a scoreboard. It must never log streaks, compare sessions, or report "you stopped early". Copy here needs a voice pass.
